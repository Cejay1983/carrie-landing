What this site collects

Very little. This site is a set of pages with links, free resources, and a way to book a call.

  • Booking a call: when you book, you enter your name, email, and phone into our scheduling tool (TaxNitro). We use that to hold your appointment and follow up about the call you booked.
  • The checklist: your checkmarks are saved on your own device only, in your browser. They are not sent to us.
  • The quiz: your answers are used on the spot to show you a result. They are not stored or sent to us.
  • Contact form and email: if you message us through our main website or by email, we keep what you send so we can reply.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't run advertising trackers on this site.
  • We don't send marketing texts or emails unless you've opted in or booked with us.
